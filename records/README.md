# The Correspondence Registry

Professional Hello World now retains administrative correspondence alongside its software. A repository capable of giving empty space a department must also be able to receive mail on that department's behalf.

These records are part of the project's artistic satire of software ceremony. Their fictional offices, contact details, charges, identifiers and procedural threats confer no actual administrative authority. Technical findings and project history must remain accurate; the joke concerns the process, not the contributor.

## Standing correspondence format

All future project correspondence follows this structure, in English. The logo, colours and case-specific content may evolve; the arrangement remains the institutional constant.

1. Place the logo beside the project name and directorate heading. Match both heading lines to the logo's principal background colour. Include the date and identify the fictional, satirical administration.
2. On the first page, provide a left administrative column: a case-number label and an extravagantly long identifier spanning its width; a rule in the logo colour; a contact label; a boxed telephone number with an explicit fictional call rate; and a postal-contact label followed by a surreal cosmic address on exactly ten lines. The inaugural record uses `1234` and `EUR 0.99/second`.
3. Set the main letter to the right of that column, with addressee, file reference, subject, salutation, reasoned body and closing. Distinguish verified reasons, additional observations and invented ceremony. Never invent a failed check or a technical defect to strengthen a joke.
4. Deliberately carry one to four lines of the closing onto an otherwise almost blank final page. The approved example carries three lines. The repeating footer does not count toward this body-text limit.
5. Repeat an administrative footer at the bottom of every page, separated by a rule in the logo colour. Include "Reserved for administrative use only", excessively precise fictional routing and retention details, sources where applicable, page numbering, a QR code and a barcode. The codes are ceremonial: encode inert text rather than a payment, tracking or operational action.
6. Verify every rendered page for legibility, clipping, spacing, the final-page overflow and correct pagination before delivery. Preserve previously issued PDFs as historical records rather than restyling them to match a later identity.

The approved PDF contains its own logo. A standalone asset library, editable layout template and automated correspondence service are not introduced by this record.

The greeting remains brief. Its outgoing mail is under no equivalent obligation.
